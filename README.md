### Q1. ​How does average shipping delay vary across states, and which geographic regions face critical fulfillment bottlenecks?

Business Objective: 
To evaluate shipping/delivery efficiency across the Central region and identify specific states suffering from very long delivery periods. This helps pinpoint fulfillment bottlenecks to improve customer satisfaction and optimize logistics.

Key Insights & Recommendations: 
Based on our data analysis of the Central region, **Indiana** and **Illinois** are experiencing the most critical fulfilment delays, with the highest average shipping times of approximately about 4.3 days per order. 
In contrast, states like **North Dakota** and **Nebraska** have highly efficient fulfilment processes, averaging just **3.3** and **3.5 days**, respectively. 

**Recommendation:** The supply chain team should immediately review the centre of  operations and shipping carrier contracts in Indiana and Illinois to resolve these bottlenecks and match the performance of faster states.

### Q2: What is the geographic distribution of profitability, and which states drive significant net losses?

Business Objective:
To map the overall profitability across the Central region and pinpoint specific states that are generating negative returns (net losses). Identifying these areas allows the business to restructure pricing, evaluate high discount rates, or reduce operational costs in underperforming regions.

Key Insights & Recommendations: 
Our geographic analysis reveals a stark contrast in regional profitability. **Texas** and **Illinois** are driving significant net losses for the company, with Texas losing over **-$25,700** and Illinois losing approximately **-$12,600**. 
Conversely, states like **Michigan** and **Indiana** are highly profitable, generating over $24,400 and $18,300 respectively. 

**Recommendation:** Management should urgently investigate the pricing and discount strategies applied in Texas and Illinois, as current strategies in these states are severely hurting the bottom line.

### Q3: What is the operational volume distribution across shipping modes, and how does each fulfillment type contribute to bottom-line profit?

Business Objective: 
To understand customer preferences regarding fulfilment methods and evaluate how each shipping mode impacts overall profitability. This analysis helps optimize logistics costs, refine shipping pricing models, and promote the most cost-effective delivery options.

Key Insights & Recommendations: 
Data from the Central region shows that **Standard Class** dominates operational volume (accounting for over 1,430 orders) and drives the bulk of total profit (+$25,350). 
Interestingly, **Second Class** shipping yields the highest efficiency, generating the most profit per order (~$19.6/order). Conversely, premium options like **First Class** and **Same Day** have lower adoption and significantly lower profit margins per order (around $12.4 to $12.7). 

**Recommendation:** The business should incentivize customers to choose Second Class and Standard Class shipping. Additionally, management must review the pricing structure and carrier contracts for First Class and Same Day deliveries to ensure premium shipping costs aren't eroding our profit margins.
